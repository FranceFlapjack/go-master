## The game

The Yomiuri newspaper paid for it to mark its twenty-thousandth issue: the young Go Seigen, a 5 dan from China, against Honinbo Shusai, the Meijin, the last holder of the old title. Fourteen sessions, three and a half months, no komi.

## What to look for

**Moves 1, 3, 5 — the opening that made the game famous.** The 3-3 point, then the 4-4 point, then *tengen*, the centre of the board. Wikipedia's article is blunt about how unusual this was: such an opening "had never before been witnessed in a professional game". Each move on its own is ordinary; the three together are a statement — Black is not going to play the corner-by-corner game the older school played.

**Moves 5–20, a framework instead of territory.** By move 13 Black has stones at D4, D10, K4 and K10: the fourth line and the centre, everything facing outwards. White answers by taking the corners and then the left side underneath. This is the *influence against territory* trade from the strategy track, played out over a whole board by two of the strongest players who ever lived.

**Move 160, H8.** White drops a stone into the middle of Black's area on the lower left. Wikipedia calls it the brilliant move that brought White back into the game — and records the rumour, never confirmed and never denied, that Shusai's student Maeda Nobuaki found it during one of the adjournments. Compare the board at move 159 with the board at move 200 and you can see what the stone did: the black area is cut into pieces.

**The adjournments.** Shusai held the right to adjourn the game at will, and used it more than a dozen times, always at his own turn to move. The practice is why modern professional games are sealed and timed.

**The end.** White by two points.

## Remember

- An opening can be a statement: 3-3, 4-4, tengen said that the centre would decide the game.
- Influence against territory, at the highest level, over a whole board and three months.
- Even in a famous game, the record is a set of facts; the story around it is a story, and this one is disputed.
